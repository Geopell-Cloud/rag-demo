# Geopell Cloud DevOps, Cloud & AI Training
## Frequently Asked Questions Knowledge Base

### Program Overview

**What is the Geopell Cloud DevOps, Cloud & AI Engineering Training?**

The Geopell Cloud DevOps, Cloud & AI Engineering Training is a 12-week instructor-led, hands-on bootcamp designed to provide practical skills in modern DevOps, cloud engineering, automation, CI/CD, containers, Infrastructure as Code, and AI-powered engineering.

The program combines technical instruction with hands-on exercises, demonstrations, troubleshooting, projects, and career-focused guidance.

---

**Who is this training for?**

The training is suitable for beginners and experienced IT professionals, including:

- Individuals transitioning into technology
- System administrators
- Cloud engineers
- Software developers
- Infrastructure engineers
- QA and support engineers
- DevOps professionals looking to expand their skills
- Professionals interested in Cloud, DevOps, automation, and AI

Previous DevOps experience is not required, but participants should be prepared to practice outside class.

## AI Engineering

**Why is AI included in DevOps training?**

Modern engineering teams increasingly use AI for software development, troubleshooting, automation, documentation, code assistance, operations, and knowledge management.

The program helps participants understand how AI can complement traditional DevOps and Cloud engineering skills.

---

**Will I learn Generative AI?**

Relevant Generative AI concepts may include:

- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Prompt engineering
- Embeddings
- Vector databases/search
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Agentic AI
- AI APIs
- AI-powered automation

---

**What is RAG?**

RAG stands for **Retrieval-Augmented Generation**.

It allows an AI application to retrieve relevant information from documents or other knowledge sources before generating an answer.

Participants can learn how document ingestion, chunking, embeddings, vector search, APIs, and LLMs work together in a RAG architecture.

---

**What is Agentic AI?**

Agentic AI refers to AI systems capable of performing multi-step tasks using models, tools, APIs, retrieval systems, reasoning, and automated workflows.

Participants may learn how agentic workflows can interact with RAG systems, APIs, cloud resources, and automation technologies.
